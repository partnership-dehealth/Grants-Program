## Project Overview :page_facing_up:

### Overview
- **Project Name:** DeHealth - Decentralized Health Data Management Platform
- **Tagline:** Empowering individuals with blockchain-based health data ownership and incentivization.
- **Project Description:**
  - DeHealth is a **blockchain-powered platform** enabling users to securely **store, access, and share medical records** while earning **tokenized rewards** for wellness engagement.
  - Integrates **smart contracts, AI-driven analytics, and privacy-preserving security** to improve **healthcare interoperability and compliance (HIPAA & GDPR).**
  - **Built on Cronos Chain & The Graph**, supporting **real-time decentralized health data querying and indexing.**
- **Why Polkadot/Kusama?**
  - Enables **scalability and interoperability** across healthcare networks.
  - Utilizes **substrate-based smart contracts for secure patient data access.**

### Project Details
- **Technology Stack:**
  - **Blockchain:** Cronos Chain
  - **Smart Contracts:** Solidity (Data Access & Tokenized Incentives)
  - **Data Indexing:** The Graph (Decentralized Querying)
  - **AI & Machine Learning:** Python-based predictive analytics
  - **Frontend & Backend:** React, Node.js
- **Core Components:**
  - **Decentralized Data Storage:** Secure, tamper-proof medical record management.
  - **Smart Contracts for Permissions:** Automates access control for healthcare providers.
  - **Tokenized Incentives:** Rewards users for wellness participation and data sharing.
  - **AI-Powered Health Insights:** Analyzes anonymized health data for research.

### Ecosystem Fit
- **Target Audience:** Healthcare providers, insurers, researchers, and individuals seeking secure data control.
- **Needs Addressed:** Reduces inefficiencies, enhances security, and improves data-sharing across medical networks.
- **Competitors & Differentiation:**
  - Competes with **Medicalchain, Patientory, BurstIQ** but differentiates by integrating **incentive mechanisms, AI-powered insights, and fully decentralized data access.**
